US History Timeline

Key events in American history from colonization to modern day.

1607

Jamestown established - the first permanent English settlement in North America.

1620

Pilgrims arrive at Plymouth Rock aboard the Mayflower; Mayflower Compact signed.

1754-1763

French and Indian War - British victory leads to increased taxation of colonies.

1765

Stamp Act passed - "No taxation without representation" becomes a rallying cry.

1773

Boston Tea Party - colonists protest British tea taxes by dumping tea into Boston Harbor.

1776

Declaration of Independence signed - the 13 colonies declare freedom from Britain.

1787

Constitutional Convention - the US Constitution is drafted in Philadelphia.

1803

Louisiana Purchase - Jefferson doubles the size of the United States.

1861-1865

Civil War - conflict between the Union and Confederate states over slavery and states' rights.

1865

13th Amendment ratified - slavery abolished throughout the United States.

1869

Transcontinental Railroad completed - connects East and West coasts.

1898

Spanish-American War - US emerges as a global imperial power.

1917

US enters World War I - shifts the balance of power in Europe.

1929

Stock Market Crash - begins the Great Depression.

1941

Pearl Harbor attacked - US enters World War II.

1954

Brown v. Board of Education - Supreme Court rules segregation in schools unconstitutional.

1964

Civil Rights Act signed - prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, or national origin.

1969

Moon Landing - Neil Armstrong becomes the first person to walk on the moon.

1974

Nixon resigns - Watergate scandal leads to the only presidential resignation in US history.

1991

Cold War ends - dissolution of the Soviet Union.

2001

September 11 attacks - leads to the War on Terror.
